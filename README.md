# HR Employee Attrition Analysis | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **employee attrition and workforce patterns** using **Power BI**. The goal is to identify factors associated with employee turnover and provide meaningful HR insights through an interactive dashboard.

The analysis covers employee demographics, job roles, overtime, satisfaction, compensation, tenure, and other workforce attributes.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Employee attrition can lead to increased recruitment costs, productivity loss, and workforce planning challenges.

The objective of this project is to analyze employee data and identify patterns that can help HR teams better understand:

* Overall employee attrition
* Departments and job roles with higher attrition
* Relationship between overtime and attrition
* Employee satisfaction and work-life balance
* Compensation and tenure patterns
* Demographic factors associated with employee turnover

---

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### Department Analysis

Analyzed employee attrition across different departments to identify areas with higher turnover.

### Job Role Analysis

Compared attrition across different job roles to identify roles requiring closer attention.

### Overtime Analysis

Analyzed the relationship between employee overtime and attrition.

### Employee Satisfaction

Examined job satisfaction, environment satisfaction, and work-life balance in relation to employee turnover.

### Compensation Analysis

Analyzed monthly income and other compensation-related factors to understand their relationship with attrition.

### Tenure Analysis

Examined years at company, years in current role, years since last promotion, and years with current manager.

### Demographic Analysis

Analyzed employee characteristics such as age and gender to identify attrition patterns.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

* The overall employee attrition rate is **16.12%**.
* **237 employees** have left the organization in the dataset.
* Attrition varies across departments and job roles.
* Overtime is an important dimension when analyzing employee turnover.
* Employee satisfaction, compensation, and tenure provide additional perspectives for understanding attrition patterns.
